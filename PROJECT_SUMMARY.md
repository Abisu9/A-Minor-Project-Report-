## Technical Specifications

**System Description:**
A web-based application for assessing drinking water quality by analyzing pH, TDS, turbidity, hardness, and chlorine levels against WHO/EPA standards.

**Architecture:** Three-tier (Presentation, Application, Data layers)

**Technology Stack:**
- Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap, Chart.js
- Backend: Python, Flask/Django, SQLAlchemy
- Database: SQLite/MySQL/PostgreSQL

**Key Features:**
- Real-time assessment
- Historical tracking
- Report generation
- Data visualization
- Educational content
